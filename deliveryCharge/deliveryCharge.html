An online store calculates delivery charges based on the customer's order amount and delivery distance. Write a JavaScript function called deliveryCharge(amount, distance) that returns the delivery fee according to these rules: If the order amount is ₦50,000 or more, delivery is free regardless of distance. Otherwise, if the distance is 5 km or less, the delivery fee is ₦1,500. Otherwise, if the distance is greater than 5 km but less than or equal to 15 km, the delivery fee is ₦3,000. Otherwise, the delivery fee is ₦5,000.
